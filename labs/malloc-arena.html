Three rounds — the lab, by hand

  1. Headers & Pointers — encode/decode the header word; payload2header, header2footer, next/prev_chunk_header.
  2. Malloc — first-fit search and the split decision (first_fit, split, mm_malloc).
  3. Heap Doctor — spot the broken invariant (mm_checkheap).

Chunk = [header 8B | payload | footer 8B], size a multiple of 16, status in bit 0 (1 = allocated, 0 = free).
